動名詞　Level 1-1

アルファ英語教材｜本文中の下線①〜④について、各問に答えなさい。

Running a small bakery involves making difficult decisions every day.

At the end of each day, the owner sometimes had bread left over.

① Throwing away unsold bread bothered her because some of it was still fresh.

② She avoided wasting food whenever possible.

She contacted a local food bank to ask whether it could accept the extra bread.

③ A staff member thanked her for offering to help.

They agreed that someone would collect the bread every Friday.

After a few weeks, ④ the owner looked forward to seeing the volunteers who came to collect it.

She was pleased that less food was going to waste.

語注

bakery：パン屋left over：余ったunsold：売れ残ったfood bank：食品を必要な人へ届ける団体volunteer：ボランティア

設問

Q1.

下線部①を和訳し、Throwingで始まる句が文の主語・目的語のどちらか答えなさい。

Q2.

下線部②を和訳し、avoidedの後ろに続く動詞の形を答えなさい。

Q3.

下線部③を和訳し、offeringの直前の前置詞を答えなさい。

Q4.

下線部④を和訳し、seeingの直前のtoが不定詞のto・前置詞のtoのどちらか答えなさい。

Q5.

次の（　）内の動詞を適切な形に直しなさい。The owner avoided ( waste ) food.

Q6.

本文の内容と一致するものを1つ選びなさい。

ア　The bakery owner gave away every loaf of bread at closing time.

イ　The food bank agreed to collect bread once a week.

ウ　The owner stopped baking bread altogether.

エ　The volunteers came to the bakery every morning.

解答

Q1　売れ残ったパンを捨てること／主語

Q2　彼女は可能な限り食品を無駄にしないようにした／-ing形（動名詞）

Q3　手助けを申し出たことに対して／for

Q4　彼女はパンを取りに来るボランティアたちに会うのを楽しみにするようになった／前置詞のto

Q5　wasting

Q6　イ
