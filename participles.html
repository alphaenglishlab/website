分詞　Level 1-1

アルファ英語教材｜本文中の下線①〜④について、各問に答えなさい。

A local museum reopened on Saturday after several months of repairs.

① Visitors waiting outside the entrance were allowed in at ten.

Inside, they saw several paintings that had been carefully restored.

② One painting damaged by water had taken nearly a year to repair.

③ A guide standing beside it explained how the work was done.

Near the exit, ④ the museum gave visitors printed cards with information about the paintings.

Many visitors said they wanted to return.

語注

reopen：再開するrestore：修復するdamaged：損傷したentrance：入口nearly：ほぼ

設問

Q1.

下線部①を和訳し、waitingが現在分詞・過去分詞のどちらか答えなさい。

Q2.

下線部②を和訳し、damagedが現在分詞・過去分詞のどちらか答えなさい。

Q3.

下線部③を和訳し、standingが修飾する名詞を答えなさい。

Q4.

下線部④を和訳し、printedが現在分詞・過去分詞のどちらか答えなさい。

Q5.

下線部①のwaiting以下を関係代名詞を使った節に書き換えなさい（wereを使うこと）。

Q6.

本文の内容と一致するものを1つ選びなさい。

ア　The museum reopened after repairs.

イ　Visitors were allowed in before nine.

ウ　Every painting had been damaged by water.

エ　The guide told visitors that the museum would close permanently.

解答

Q1　入口の外で待っていた来館者たち／現在分詞

Q2　水で損傷した1枚の絵／過去分詞

Q3　そのそばに立っている案内係／guide

Q4　印刷されたカード／過去分詞

Q5　Visitors who were waiting outside the entrance

Q6　ア
