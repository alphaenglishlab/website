関係代名詞　Level 1-1

アルファ英語教材｜本文中の下線①〜④について、各問に答えなさい。

On Monday morning, a customer brought an old bicycle to a repair shop.

① The mechanic who greeted him noticed that the front wheel was damaged.

② The bicycle that the customer had found in his garage had not been used for years.

The mechanic inspected it carefully and made a list of the parts she needed.

③ She called a supplier whose store was nearby.

Fortunately, the supplier had everything she needed.

④ The parts she ordered arrived that afternoon.

By closing time, the bicycle was ready to ride again.

語注

mechanic：修理工front wheel：前輪inspect：点検するsupplier：部品などの供給業者by closing time：閉店時刻までに

設問

Q1.

下線部①を和訳し、whoが関係詞節の中で主語・目的語のどちらの働きをしているか答えなさい。

Q2.

下線部②を和訳し、thatが関係詞節の中で主語・目的語のどちらの働きをしているか答えなさい。

Q3.

下線部③を和訳し、whoseの直後に置かれている名詞を抜き出しなさい。

Q4.

下線部④を和訳し、省略されている目的格の関係代名詞として使える語を2つ答えなさい。

Q5.

次の2文を関係代名詞を用いて1文にしなさい。The mechanic called a supplier. The supplier's store was nearby.

Q6.

本文の内容と一致するものを1つ選びなさい。

ア　The customer had bought the bicycle the day before.

イ　The mechanic ordered parts from a nearby supplier.

ウ　The supplier delivered the parts the following week.

エ　The customer decided not to repair the bicycle.

解答

Q1　彼にあいさつした修理工／主語（whoはgreetedの主語）。

Q2　その客が自宅のガレージで見つけた自転車／目的語（thatはhad foundの目的語）。

Q3　店が近くにある供給業者／store

Q4　彼女が注文した部品／that、which

Q5　The mechanic called a supplier whose store was nearby.

Q6　イ
